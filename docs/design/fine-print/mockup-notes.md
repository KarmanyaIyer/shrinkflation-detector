# Direction 4: The Fine Print
- Direction: a graphics-desk data story whose headline is the finding (38 prices rose, 36 fell, 3 packages shrank in 16 days), told through one pinned graphic over 6 scroll steps, then handed to the tools: ask, every change, search, method box, footnotes.
- Interactive: scroll-driven graphic (hover or tap any of the 1,242 dots for a tooltip, click to open its history); ask box (free text or example links, 2.1 s pending log that names the real tool call, then the recorded answer with product names that open the drawer, plus a "How this answer was found" disclosure and model/tokens/latency/trace footnote); change table with kind filter, category select, sort by product, unit price change or date, and "Show all 78"; client-side search over 1,242 names; side drawer with an annotated step chart of unit price, a stored-states table and a one-sentence summary generated from the data (Esc closes, focus trapped).
- Signature animation: dots tween 700 ms ease-in-out: unit chart by category, the 78 changes light up (per-category counts appear), they fly into a unit-price beeswarm with a broken axis for the shrinks and leader-line notes on Kodiak and Reese's, then land on package cards where the "after" box shrinks from the dashed "before" height, then back to the overview. Reduced motion: end states with a crossfade.
- Fonts: Newsreader (headline, body, 18 to 20 px), Public Sans (charts, labels, UI), system mono only for tool names. Palette only: #FFFFFF, ink #121212, rules #DADADA, secondary #555555, vermilion #E4572E (costs more), teal #17827A (costs less), neutral #C4C4C4.
- Data: all numbers come from MOCK/data, embedded inline (no runtime fetch). Only external request: Google Fonts. Dates are in America/New_York.
- Gaps: canvas dots are not keyboard-focusable (the table and search give keyboard access to every product). The ask always replays the one recorded exchange. Unchanged products have no unit price in field.json, so their drawer shows only the label and price. Products outside the six history files use their before/after change record.
- Screens: full-page shots show the pinned graphic only once; each step is in extra-scrolly-desktop.png and extra-scrolly-mobile.png. extra-motion-frames.png = a mid-flight frame (grid to beeswarm) and the ask pending state. mobile-full.png is at dpr 1 because dpr 2 exceeds Chrome's 16k capture height and wraps.
- Source: _build/src.html is the template; `python3 _build/build.py` inlines the data into index.html.

Grocery prices

Over 16 days at one Kroger, 38 prices rose, 36 fell and three packages shrank.

The biggest shrink was a pack of Huggies baby wipes. Its label went from “192 ct” to “56 ct” at the same $6.99.

By Karmanya Iyer·Checked every morning at 7 a.m. Eastern

A scheduled job records the package size and shelf price of 1,242 grocery products at one Cincinnati-area Kroger2, through Kroger’s public product API. It reads each size from the label text, divides price by size to get a price per unit, and stores a new record only when the size or price changes.

Prices moved in both directions, almost evenly: 38 went up and 36 came down. Of those 74 moves, 57 were under 10% per unit.

Three packages shrank while the shelf price stayed the same.

Two are multipack labels whose text changed: Dove lost its “2 pk” and Philadelphia lost the word “each”. That is what the label says, not a measurement of the package.1

One package grew. Khloud protein popcorn went from 4 oz to 5 oz at the same $5.99, so its price per ounce fell 20%.

    How this works

    1. Collection. Every morning at 11:00 UTC, which is 7 a.m. Eastern, a job asks Kroger’s public product API for all 1,242 products in 25 API calls. The Sep 23 run checked every product in 6 minutes 41 seconds with no errors.
    2. Reading labels. Each label, such as “12 oz”, “2 pk / 12 fl oz” or “192 ct”, becomes a quantity and a unit. Plain labels are parsed by rules. Hard ones go to DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, which has handled 433 of them for $0.08 in total. Every result is validated with Pydantic before it is stored.
    3. Unit price. The regular price divided by the quantity, in the product’s own unit: per oz, per fl oz or per count. Promo prices are recorded but not used.
    4. Storage. A new row is stored only when the size or price differs from the last check. 1,370 rows hold the full history of 1,242 products.
    5. Publishing. A change is published once a later check sees the new size or price. There are 78 so far: 38 price increases, 36 price cuts, 3 shrinks and 1 grow. A fifth kind, shrank but cheaper per unit, has no records yet.
    6. The assistant. It can only call four read-only tools: search_products, get_product_history, list_recent_changes and get_tracking_stats. Each call is traced with OpenTelemetry.
